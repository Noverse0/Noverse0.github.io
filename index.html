I am an AI Engineer at Ensapia (formerly Cocone), AI Lab, in Seoul, Korea. I received my M.S. in Data Science from KAIST, advised by Prof. Mun Yong Yi, and hold a B.A. in Economics and a B.S. in Information and Communication Technology from Handong Global University.

At Ensapia I designed and built the company's production agentic RAG system for enterprise knowledge search. Watching that system fail in deployment — confident hallucination, and multi-hop breakdowns that stayed invisible because the final answer still looked plausible — is what moved me from building these systems to studying how they should be controlled at runtime. That question now drives my independent first-author research, which I continue alongside full-time work.

Decision-aware LLM systems — reliability without wasted computation: when to retrieve, read, verify, stop, or use a smaller model.

Research Interests

  • Runtime control for LLM agents — when to stop searching, and whether the retrieved evidence was actually read (HALT, READ-GATE)
  • Evaluation beyond accuracy — trajectory-level failure decomposition
  • RAGOps & efficient serving — confining expensive LLM reasoning to offline stages
